> also add this and explain more so can learn that and use that : BM25 (Best Matching 25) is a search-ranking algorithm. It helps a search engine find documents that are most relevant to your query by looking at the words they contain.
>
> It's one of the fundamental algorithms behind traditional text search engines.
>
> Think of it as the technology behind searching for a phrase in Google or a document management system, before considering semantic AI search.

> BM25 vs. Vector Search
> This is the most important distinction for your RAG architecture.
>
> | BM25 | Vector search |
> |---|---|
> | Matches words | Matches meanings |
> | Excellent for exact terminology | Excellent for semantic similarity |
> | No embedding model required | Requires embedding model |
> | Finds VM-023 precisely | May understand "ventilation motor" and "air handling unit" are related |
> | Lexical search | Semantic search |

> Hybrid search
> User question: Why does VM-023 repeatedly overheat?
> BM25: Find exact equipment ID and terms
> Vector: Find semantically related failures
> Combine results with RRF
> Rank the most useful evidence together
